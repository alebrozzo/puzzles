## Puzzle 3 — Find the missing items

Solution:

A heavy horse figurine
A substantial glass ashtray
A marble bust of Mozart
A brass candlestick

--

The reader is presented with three images of different parts of the victim's study. They are casual fotos taken a few days before. Then they see how the same areas look after the murder, with certain items missing. The challenge is to identify which items are no longer present.
