## Puzzle 4 — Detective's findings

Solution:

| Guest  | Possible motive                                | Evidence found by the detective                            | Where the evidence was found      |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Alice  | Vale was cutting her out of their business     | A letter to the board of directors                         | Desk drawer                       |
| Robert | Sold Vale a forged item                        | An certificate of a painting claiming it's not an original | Inside a folder in a file cabinet |
| Carl   | Vale would retract a false alibi               | A newspaper clipping with a handwritten text on the back   | Pants front pocket                |
| Daniel | Needed funds that Vale would leave in his will | Vale's will                                                | Inside the safe                   |

Later on we will say that the newspaper clipping was on his pocket because he showed it to Carl saying he'd be retracting his statement

---

Guests: Alice, Robert, Carl, Daniel
Possible motives: losing business (can be Alice or Daniel), Retract alibi, Needed money, Forged piece of art (replaced existing?)
Evidence: Letter, Will, Newspaper clipping, Certificate
Where: Safe, Desk, Cabinet, Clothing

---

Narration:
----------

"I'm done looking at the murder scene," said the detective "and I am surprised about how _much_ I've found. Usually after someone goes through the victim's belongings we have trouble finding clues, yet here we've found more clues than any of my previous cases."

"I imagine all of it in Alexander's safe?" asked Robert

"Strangely, no." replied the detective. "Only his will was there. Well, along with some cash but that is no evidence. Of tax evasion maybe, but not helpful to this case. What I did find that involves you, was in the cabinet."
_Clue: the will was in the safe_
_Clue: Robert + file cabinet_

"What could you possible find about me? I loved the old man!"

"Well I doubt most of us were in his will so you have your killer right there, don't you." interrupted Alice, looking directly at Carl.

"What!?" squeaked Carl "I had NO reason to kill him! I am not even in his will! You are the one that was going to be ditched from your own company, but now you get it all!"
_Clue: Alice was being cut off the company_

"I was going to be WHAT??!!" yelled Alice even louder. "Is that what the will says?" she continued, looking at the detective.

"Not the will, no." he answered, "But it is true."
_Clue: Alice not will, cut out of business not will_

"And Daniel has very good motives as well," restarted Carl, now more calmed, and even smiling. "Don't, you, doctor?"
_Clue: Daniel + will_

"You absolute piece of... something!" the doctor reacted. "Like you are such a great citizen! How about that accident you caused that your uncle fixed for you! You'd be in jail now if it wasn't for him providing you with a fake alibi! Everyone here knows it!"
_Clue: Carl + alibi_

"I didn't know..." stupidly added the art dealer. Everybody looked at him immediately. No-one said anything for a few seconds, as if expecting him to throw more dirty laundry. He had nothing to add.

"Say, didn't you sell my uncle some bogus painting? He told me all about it. We talked a lot, him and I, because with no money bonds between us, we could have a real relation. Detective Thorne, you probably found an certificate of... not real art, in his desk drawers?"
_Clue: Robert + forgery_

"All I found there was an unrelated yet interesting letter" the detective replied. "But somewhere else, I also found a Newspaper clipping related to you, so everyone here is still a suspect."
_Clue: letter + drawer_
